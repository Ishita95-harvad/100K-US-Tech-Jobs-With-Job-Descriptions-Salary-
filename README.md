# 💻 100K US Tech Jobs – Job Descriptions & Salary Data

## 📌 Overview
This dataset contains **100,000 US-based tech job listings**, covering **roles, job descriptions, skill requirements, and salary insights**. It is designed for **data analysts, job seekers, HR professionals, and workforce researchers** to analyze hiring trends in the tech industry.

## 🔍 Dataset Description
Key attributes in the dataset include:
- **Job Title** – Role designation (e.g., Software Engineer, Data Scientist).
- **Company Name & Location** – Employer details & geographic hiring patterns.
- **Skill Requirements** – Technical & soft skills listed in job postings.
- **Experience Level** – Entry-level, mid-level, senior positions.
- **Salary Range** – Minimum & maximum pay estimates per job listing.
- **Job Description** – Detailed responsibilities & qualifications.
- **Industry & Specialization** – Cloud computing, AI, cybersecurity, fintech, etc.

## 📊 Usage & Applications
- **Salary Benchmarking** – Compare wages across tech roles and industries.
- **Hiring Trend Analysis** – Explore demand for various tech skills.
- **Predictive Modeling** – Forecast job market trends based on historical data.
- **Job Recommendation Engine** – AI-driven insights for career planning.
- **Competitive Hiring Strategies** – HR professionals can optimize recruitment.
